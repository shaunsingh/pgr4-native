# Native 16:10 (1280x800): what it takes

Notes from a probe on 2026-10-24 (td-222). PGR4 renders 1280x720; on the Deck's 1280x800 panel
the native renderer shows it letterboxed, never stretched. Rendering 1280x800 natively is a
game patch, not a renderer setting.

## Where the size lives

- Device creation, `sub_82317D48` (Title Update #2): `li r11,1280` / `li r11,720` at
  0x82317D80 / 0x82317D98 are the presentation parameters' BackBufferWidth/Height
  (`stw r11,44(r31)`, `stw r11,48(r31)`), after the call to XGetVideoMode. `li r3,1280` /
  `li r4,720` at 0x82317E74 / 0x82317E6C size the front buffer texture (`sub_822B40F8`).
- td-222 set both heights to 800 through mid-asm hooks. The front buffer allocation moved
  (resolve destination 1F1A8000 instead of 1F1F8000), but the frame was unchanged: the scene,
  its resolves and the post-processing chain keep their own sizes, and the image was still
  1280x720, letterboxed.
- The generated code has 38 `li rN,720` and 43 `li rN,1280` sites, 14 of them within 30
  instructions of each other. Float constants (the projection's 16:9, 1/1280 and 1/720 texel
  sizes) come from memory and are not in that count.

## The frame (td-208's draw log)

- Scene: 1280x720, 2x MSAA, in two predicated tiles (rows 0-480 and 480-720, EDRAM color at
  0, depth at tile 960). 800 rows fit the same two tiles (480 + 320).
- Post-processing at 1/4 (320x180), 1/8 (160x90), 1/16 (80x45) and 1/32 (40x22), each a
  resolve into a guest texture of that size.
- Tone map (ps 6B06B8CDADD9CCBF) into the 1280x720 front buffer (fmt 7).

## What a patch needs

1. Every render target, viewport, scissor, clear and resolve rectangle of the scene and the
   post chain at the new height, and the guest textures they resolve into allocated for it
   (a resolve of 800 rows into an allocation of 720 overwrites whatever follows).
2. The projection: Hor+ at 16:10 keeps the horizontal field of view and adds height, or the
   vertical field of view is kept and the sides cut; the game's aspect constant decides.
3. The HUD and menus, laid out for 1280x720: anchored to the edges or centred, as
   UnleashedRecomp's `aspect_ratio_patches.cpp` does for Sonic Unleashed.
4. Photo mode, replays and the garage, which may make their own targets.

Prior art: UnleashedRecomp (aspect ratio patches, per-HUD-element anchoring); Xenia Canary's
patch files for PGR4 have no aspect patch.
